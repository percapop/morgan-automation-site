## The problem

You didn't start a business to copy prices into spreadsheets.

But somebody has to search for new buyers, retype orders from emails, update the catalogue and chase reorders. In a small team, that somebody is usually you, in the evening.

Most of this work follows the same steps every time. That makes it a good fit for a small automation: something that does the repetitive part and hands you the result to check.

---

## What I can automate for you

Every business works differently, so think of what follows as a menu of ideas, not a fixed list. Your best first project might be on it, or it might be something we only spot once we talk.

The easiest way to find out is a free 30-minute call. You tell me how your week actually goes, and together we work out what's worth automating for *you*.

Each project has one clear job and a fixed price agreed before I start. Most use AI to read, sort or draft, and a person checks the result. You can start small and add more later.

### 1. Find new customers
*Typically 1–2 weeks*

From "who should we sell to?" to a list you can start calling on Monday:

**Find → Check → Rank → Reach out → Follow up**

1. **Find.** Businesses that match your ideal buyer, such as restaurants, shops or wholesalers in a city or region.
2. **Check.** The right contact person, email, phone and website, cleaned, with duplicates removed.
3. **Rank.** Every lead scored by fit, so you start with the best ones.
4. **Reach out.** A short, personal first message for each lead, drafted with AI in English, Japanese or another language, for you to review and send.
5. **Follow up.** A simple tracker showing who replied and who needs a nudge this week.

### 2. Sales and orders
*Typically a few days to 3 weeks*

- **Order intake from email.** Orders that arrive by email, PDF or WhatsApp export are read with AI, checked and put into your order sheet, ready for you to confirm.
- **Quote and offer builder.** Fill in the customer and products and get a clean, branded quote in minutes.
- **Price lists and catalogues.** Update one source file and get up-to-date PDFs or spreadsheets for each customer group or language.
- **Product spec sheets.** Consistent spec sheets made from the product data you already have.
- **Reorder reminders.** Customers get a nudge around the time they usually reorder, and you get a list of who's overdue.
- **New customer welcome pack.** When someone signs up, they get your catalogue, terms and ordering instructions without you having to send them by hand.

### 3. Back office
*Typically 1–3 weeks*

- **Supplier invoices and documents.** Invoices, delivery notes and certificates are read with AI and their key details go into a spreadsheet, ready for your bookkeeper.
- **Weekly sales summary.** One short report every Monday: what sold, who ordered, what changed and who's gone quiet.
- **Stock and price alerts.** You get a message when stock runs low or a supplier's price changes.
- **Inbox sorting.** Incoming emails are sorted by AI into orders, questions, complaints and spam, and common questions get a draft reply for you to approve.
- **Data clean-up.** Messy customer or product lists are merged, de-duplicated and put into one tidy file.

### 4. Content and marketing
*Typically 1–2 weeks each*

- **Weekly trend scan.** What's moving in your market, including competitors, new products and seasonal demand, in five minutes of reading.
- **Product descriptions and translations.** Consistent descriptions for your shop or catalogue, drafted with AI in several languages and checked before they go live.
- **Caption drafts.** Social posts drafted with AI from your products and photos, for you to edit and approve.
- **Seasonal content calendar.** A plan for the year built around the holidays, seasons and events that matter to your customers.
- **Review monitoring.** New Google reviews are collected in one place, with a polite draft reply for each.
- **Performance report.** What worked last month, in plain language.

### 5. Websites and landing pages
*Typically 1–2 weeks*

- **Landing pages and product sites.** Front-end sites for B2B and B2C businesses: a page for a product, campaign or trade fair, or a showcase site that stays in sync with your product list.
- **Enquiry and booking forms.** Price list requests, sample orders, tastings or workshops, landing straight in your inbox or spreadsheet.

Already selling on Shopify or another platform? I'll build pages and automations around your shop, but not the shop itself.

### 6. Team workshop
*Half a day*

- **AI for your team.** A hands-on session where your team learns to use AI tools for the work they actually do, such as emails, translations, price checks and reports. Everyone leaves with something that works.

Not sure which one you need? That's what the free call is for. <!-- "free call" links to the contact section -->

## What I don't do

So you know where I'm not the right fit:

- Large company-wide systems or six-month projects
- Sending mass cold emails or messages on your behalf
- Anything that replaces your judgement with customers. AI drafts and a person decides.
- **Online shops, checkouts and payment systems.** Taking payments safely means handling card data, fraud checks, VAT, refunds and constant security updates. Platforms like Shopify do this well for a small monthly fee, and they keep it running when something breaks at 2 a.m. A custom-built shop would cost you more and be riskier. If you need to sell online, I'll point you to the right platform and build the landing pages and automations around it.

## How a project runs, start to finish

**1. Free 30-minute call.** <!-- Card has a centred [Let's talk] button linking to the contact section -->
You tell me where the time goes. I tell you honestly whether automation will help, and if it won't, I'll say so.

**2. Fixed-price proposal.**
Within a few days you get a short proposal: what I'll build, what it costs and when it'll be ready. You pay for the result, not by the hour.

**3. Build and check.**
I build it, using AI where it saves time, test it on your real data and send you short updates as I go. Nothing goes live until you've seen it work.

**4. Handover.**
You get the working automation, the code and a plain-language guide. Everything I build for you is your property: the code, the accounts and the data, with no licence fees and no lock-in. If you want, I can keep an eye on it afterwards for a small monthly fee. You don't have to.

## Questions people ask before the first call

**Do I need to be technical?**
No. If you can use email and a spreadsheet, you can use what I build. You also get a plain-language guide.

**What tools do you use?**
Whatever fits what you already have, such as Google Sheets, Excel, email or your existing shop system, plus AI models for reading, sorting and drafting. I don't make you switch.

**Is my data safe?**
Your data stays in your accounts wherever possible. I only access what the project needs, AI tools only see the parts a task needs, and I hand back access at the end.

**What if it breaks?**
Every project includes [30 days] of fixes after handover. After that, you can pick the optional monthly care or call me when you need me.

**Will AI send things to my customers without me knowing?**
No. Anything customer-facing goes through a human check first, by me during the build and by you once it's running, unless you choose otherwise.

**Do you only work in the Netherlands?**
No. I work remotely with small businesses across Europe.
