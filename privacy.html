Privacy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version: this website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. The only personal data I receive is what you send me through the contact form, and I use it only to reply to you.

Who is responsible

Perseus Morgan, trading as Morgan Automation, The Hague, Netherlands. Email: perseus@morganautomations.com.

The contact form

When you use the contact form, it sends your name, company (if you fill it in), email address and message. The form is handled by Web3Forms, a form service that passes your message on by email. It arrives in my business inbox, which runs on Google Workspace.

I use your details only to answer your enquiry and, if we work together, to run that project. I don't add you to a mailing list, and I don't sell or share your details with anyone else. The legal basis is that you asked me to get in touch (GDPR article 6(1)(b), steps taken at your request before a possible agreement).

Web3Forms and Google may process data outside the European Union, under their own privacy terms (linked above).

No cookies or tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social media tools. Fonts are stored on the site itself, so loading a page doesn't contact anyone else. If you choose a language, it's kept in the page address only (for example ?lang=nl), not stored on your device.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

How long I keep your message

If we don't start working together, I delete your enquiry within 12 months. If we do start working together, I keep what Dutch law requires for my business records. You can ask me to delete your message at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data I hold about you, to restrict or object to how I use it, or to receive a copy. Just email perseus@morganautomations.com. If you're unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If this statement changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
